# Practical 06 — PA System with Cordless Microphones

## Write-Up
**Aim:** Study a public-address system with cordless microphones.  
**Apparatus:** Microphones, mixer, power amplifiers and speakers.

## Theory
A Public Address system amplifies speech/music to cover a large area. It uses sound sources, a mixer, power amplifiers, speakers and cables. A cordless microphone radio-transmits audio to a receiver connected to the PA.

## Block Diagram

## Working
1. Microphones convert sound to electrical audio signals.
2. A cordless mic transmits its signal to the receiver.
3. Mixer balances and combines inputs.
4. Power amplifier boosts the mixed signal.
5. Speakers convert amplified electrical signals to sound.

## Wireless Microphone
The mic contains a battery-powered radio transmitter. A receiver recovers audio and connects to the sound system. Handheld and collar/lavalier types are used in speaking, broadcasting and performance.

## Advantages
- Freedom of movement.
- No cable across the stage or area.
- Reduced cable handling and trip hazards.

## Disadvantages
- Radio range is limited.
- Radio interference may occur.
- Battery runtime limits operation.

## PA Applications in Railways
Passenger updates (arrival/departure/delays); yard staff coordination; emergency announcements; special functions; workshop announcements; conferences and meetings.

## Conclusion
PA systems distribute amplified audio to an audience. Cordless microphones provide mobility through a radio link.
